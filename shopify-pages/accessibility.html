We want everyone to be able to learn about and buy Matcha Sous, including people who use assistive technology.

Our standard.

We aim for this site to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. We test the site with automated accessibility checks and by hand using only a keyboard, and we fix problems as we find them.

What we've done.

  • Pages use clear headings and structure, so they can be navigated with a screen reader or keyboard.
  • Images have text descriptions.
  • Form fields are labeled.
  • Videos that play automatically have a pause button, and they don't play at all if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • A "Skip to content" link lets keyboard users jump past the menu.
  • Text and background colors are chosen to be readable.

Checkout.

Checkout and payment are provided by Shopify, which maintains its own accessibility program for those pages.

Tell us if something doesn't work.

If you have trouble using any part of this site, or need information in a different format, email care@matchasous.com. Tell us the page and what went wrong. We reply within one business day, and if a problem is stopping you from ordering, we'll help you place your order directly.
