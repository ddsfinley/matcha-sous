Shipping & returns

Free express shipping, honest delivery estimates and a generous 30-night trial. Here is exactly what to expect from the moment you order to the unlikely event of a return.

Last updated: June 2026

Shipping

Shipping is complimentary on every Matcha Sous order — no minimums and no surprises at checkout. Orders are processed and dispatched within 1–2 business days (Monday to Friday, excluding holidays). You will receive an email confirmation when you order and a separate email with tracking once your parcel is on its way.

Delivery estimates

  • United States: typically 3–5 business days via express courier after dispatch.
  • Canada: typically 5–8 business days after dispatch.
  • United Kingdom & Europe: typically 6–10 business days after dispatch.
  • Rest of world: typically 6–12 business days after dispatch, depending on destination and local customs.

These are estimates, not guarantees. Delivery times can vary during launch periods, peak seasons and due to courier or customs delays outside our control. International orders may be subject to import duties or taxes set by your country; any such charges are the responsibility of the recipient.

Order tracking

As soon as your order ships, we will email you a tracking number and a link to follow your parcel. If you have created an account, you can also view your order status there. If your tracking has not updated within a few business days of dispatch, or you have not received tracking at all, contact us at care@matchasous.com with your order number and we will look into it right away.

The 30-Night Ritual Trial

We want you to live with Matcha Sous, not just unbox it. Every order includes a 30-night ritual trial: brew with your mixer for up to 30 nights from the date of delivery, and if it is not right for you, return it for a full refund of the purchase price. There is no need to explain why, though we always welcome your feedback so we can keep improving.

To stay eligible for the trial refund, please keep the original packaging and accessories where possible, and return the mixer in good, working condition with normal use only.

Returns & refunds

Starting a return is simple:

  • Email care@matchasous.com with your order number and let us know you would like to return your mixer.
  • We will reply with a prepaid return label and simple instructions, usually within one business day.
  • Pack the mixer securely — ideally in its original box with the pitcher, magnetic whisk, USB-C cable and ritual guide — and drop it off using the label provided.
  • Once we receive and inspect your return, we will process your refund to the original payment method within 5–10 business days. Your bank may take a few additional days to post the funds.

Returns are accepted within the 30-night trial window. Items returned outside this window, or that show damage beyond normal use, may be eligible for a partial refund or may be returned to you. Please do not send a return without contacting us first, as we cannot guarantee that unannounced returns will be processed.

2-Year Warranty

Matcha Sous is backed by a 2-year limited warranty from the date of delivery, covering defects in materials and workmanship under normal household and ordinary commercial use. If a covered defect appears, we will repair or replace the affected part or the mixer at no charge.

The warranty does not cover accidental damage, misuse, submersion of the powered base, unauthorised repairs, cosmetic wear, or normal consumable wear. To make a warranty claim, email care@matchasous.com with your order number and a short description (photos help). This warranty is in addition to, and does not affect, any rights you may have under applicable consumer law.

Damaged or incorrect items

We pack every mixer carefully, but if your order arrives damaged, defective or incorrect, please contact us within 7 days of delivery at care@matchasous.com. Include your order number and a few photos of the item and packaging. We will arrange a free replacement or a full refund as quickly as possible — no need to return a damaged item before we make it right.

Questions?

If anything here is unclear, our team is glad to help. Reach us at care@matchasous.com or through our contact page, and we will reply within one business day. You may also find quick answers on our FAQ page.
