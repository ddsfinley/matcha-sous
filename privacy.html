Privacy policy

We collect as little as we can, use it only to serve you well, and never sell it. This policy explains, in plain language, what we gather and the choices you have.

Last updated: June 2026

Introduction

This privacy policy explains how Matcha Sous (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses and protects your personal information when you visit matchasous.com, place an order, or get in touch with us. By using our website, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the site.

Information we collect

We collect information you give us and information that is gathered automatically as you browse:

  • Order and contact details you provide, such as your name, email address, shipping and billing address, and phone number.
  • Communications you send us, including messages submitted through our contact form or by email.
  • Payment information, which is collected and processed directly by our payment providers. We do not store full card numbers on our servers.
  • Technical and usage data collected automatically, such as your IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed and referring links.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process, fulfil and deliver your orders, and send order confirmations and shipping updates.
  • Provide customer support and respond to your questions, returns and warranty claims.
  • Operate, maintain, secure and improve our website and products.
  • Send marketing emails about products, offers and news where you have opted in; you can unsubscribe at any time.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, and comply with our legal obligations.

Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep your cart working, remember your preferences, understand how the site is used and measure the performance of our marketing. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, though some parts of the site may not function properly without them.

Sharing & third parties

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with trusted service providers who help us run our business, and only as needed to provide their service, including:

  • Payment processors, to securely take and verify payments.
  • Shipping and fulfilment partners, to pack and deliver your order.
  • Analytics and marketing providers, to understand site usage and reach the right audiences.
  • Authorities or other parties where required by law, or to protect our rights, safety and property.

Data retention

We keep your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, including to meet legal, accounting, tax and reporting requirements. When information is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymise it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, update, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@matchasous.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures — including encryption in transit and access controls — to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your account credentials confidential.

Children

Our website and products are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above. Significant changes may be communicated by email or a notice on our site. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or how we handle your information, please email us at privacy@matchasous.com or reach us through our contact page. We will be glad to help.
