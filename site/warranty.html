What is not covered.

This warranty does not cover normal wear; cosmetic damage; loss of the removable mixing whisk or charging cable; ordinary cable wear; physical damage to the charging cable; or damage caused by accident, misuse, abuse, overfilling, improper cleaning, dishwasher use, liquid entering the base or charging port, operation while connected to power, an incompatible or damaged charging cable, adapter, or power source, unauthorized modification or repair, failure to follow this guide, or use with milk or any substance other than matcha powder, water, and beverage syrup. For purposes of this warranty, “beverage syrup” means a clear, pourable, dairy-free flavoring syrup.

The supplied charging cable is covered only against defects in materials or workmanship. Ordinary wear, misuse, and physical damage are not covered. Damage caused by an incompatible cable, adapter, or power source is not covered.

Replacement parts purchased separately, including mixing whisks and charging cables, are warranted against defects in materials and workmanship for 90 days from the date of purchase.

Use of a third-party part or service does not by itself void this warranty. Damage caused by an incompatible part, accessory, repair, or modification is not covered. Warranty service under this warranty will be performed by Presso Inc. or a service provider it designates.
