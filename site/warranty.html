Limited warranty.

One year against manufacturing defects. Here's exactly what that covers, and what it doesn't.

This is a limited warranty, not a full warranty as that term is defined by the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act. It covers defects in materials and workmanship, with the exclusions listed below.

One year. From the day it arrives.

Every Matcha Sous is covered for one year from the delivery date against defects in materials and workmanship: the mixing base, the magnetic drive, the touch controls and the USB-C charging circuit.

Keep your order confirmation email. It's your proof of purchase, and the delivery date starts the clock.

What the warranty doesn't cover.

  • Normal wear, including matcha staining the steel over time.
  • The stainless vessel and mixing rod, if damaged by drop or impact.
  • Water damage from submerging the base. Rinse the vessel, wipe the base, never submerge it.
  • Damage from anything but matcha and water run through it, including other liquids, ice, or foreign objects.
  • Damage from a non-original charging cable, incorrect voltage, or a power surge.
  • Opening, modifying, or attempting your own repair of the base.
  • Units bought secondhand or from an unauthorized reseller. Coverage is for the original purchaser, on units bought directly from us or from a listed stockist.
  • Cosmetic wear that doesn't affect function: light scuffing, minor discoloration.

If something's actually wrong.

Write to care@matchasous.com with your order confirmation, a description of the problem, and a photo or short video if the issue is visible. We'll get back to you within one business day.

If the unit is faulty through no fault of your own, we'll repair it, replace it, or refund what you paid, whichever gets you back to a working Sous soonest. That choice is ours to make.

The legal fine print.

This warranty is in addition to any implied warranties that apply under the law of your state, including the implied warranty of merchantability. We do not disclaim those. We can't. Where the law allows it, implied warranties on this product are limited to the same one-year duration as the warranty above. Some states don't allow a limit on how long an implied warranty lasts, or the exclusion of incidental or consequential damages, so that limitation may not apply to you.

To the extent the law allows, we're not liable for incidental or consequential damages arising from a defect, such as lost matcha or lost time. This warranty gives you specific legal rights, and you may have other rights that vary from state to state.

This warranty applies to units shipped within the United States. See the FAQ for current shipping, cancellation and pre-order terms.
