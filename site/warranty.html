Limited warranty.

One year against manufacturing defects. Here's exactly what that covers, and what it doesn't.

This is a limited warranty, not a full warranty as that term is defined by the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act. It extends only to the original purchaser of a Matcha Sous bought directly from matchasous.com or from an authorized stockist, for use within the United States.

Proof of purchase, such as your order confirmation email, is required before any warranty service is performed. Our obligation under this warranty is limited to repairing the unit, replacing it, or refunding what you paid, at our option. All claims must be preauthorized: write to us before sending anything back. No warranty service will be provided for a unit that has been opened, modified, or otherwise tampered with, unless we've directed you to do so.

One year. From the day it arrives.

Every Matcha Sous is covered for one year from the delivery date against defects in materials and workmanship: the mixing base, the magnetic drive, the touch controls and the USB-C charging circuit.

Keep your order confirmation email. It's your proof of purchase, and the delivery date starts the clock.

What the warranty doesn't cover.

  • Normal wear, including matcha staining the steel over time.
  • Damage from negligent use, misuse, or use contrary to the instructions in the product manual.
  • The stainless mixing cup and mixing rod, if damaged by drop or impact.
  • Water damage from submerging the base. Rinse the mixing cup, wipe the base, never submerge it.
  • Damage from anything but matcha and water run through it, including other liquids, ice, or foreign objects.
  • Damage from a non-original charging cable, incorrect voltage, or a power surge.
  • Disassembly, repair, or alteration by anyone other than us, unless we've directed it.
  • Units bought secondhand or from an unauthorized reseller. Coverage is for the original purchaser, on units bought directly from us or from a listed stockist.
  • Damage from fire, flood, or other events beyond our control.
  • Cosmetic wear that doesn't affect function: light scuffing, minor discoloration.

If something's actually wrong.

Write to care@matchasous.com with your order confirmation, a description of the problem, and a photo or short video if the issue is visible. Do not send anything back before hearing from us. We'll get back to you within one business day.

If we determine the unit is defective under the terms of this warranty, we'll repair it, replace it, or refund what you paid, whichever gets you back to a working Sous soonest, and we'll cover the cost of return shipping. That choice is ours to make. If we determine the issue isn't covered, we'll tell you before any work is done, and return shipping is yours to arrange.
