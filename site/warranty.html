Limited warranty.

One year against manufacturing defects, plainly stated. This page is the full version of what your manual points you to.

This is a limited warranty, not a full warranty as that term is defined by the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act. It covers defects in materials and workmanship. It does not cover everything, and the exclusions below are part of the warranty, not fine print working against it.

One year. From the day it arrives.

Every Matcha Sous is covered for one year from the delivery date against defects in materials and workmanship: the mixing base, the magnetic drive, the touch controls and the USB-C charging circuit failing to do what they were built to do, through no fault of your own.

Keep your order confirmation email. It is your proof of purchase and the delivery date is what starts the clock.

What the warranty doesn't cover.

  • Normal wear. Matcha stains steel over time. A base that looks lived-in after a year of daily use is not a defect.
  • The stainless vessel and mixing rod, if damaged by drop or impact. They are dishwasher-durable, not indestructible.
  • Submerging the base. The manual and this site both say the same thing: rinse the vessel, wipe the base, never submerge it. Water damage from ignoring that isn't a manufacturing defect.
  • Anything but matcha and water run through it. Other liquids, ice, or foreign objects in the vessel void this section.
  • Damage from a non-original charging cable, incorrect voltage, or a power surge.
  • Opening, modifying, or attempting your own repair of the base. There's nothing serviceable inside it, and doing so ends this warranty's coverage for the unit.
  • Units bought secondhand or from a reseller we didn't authorize. This warranty covers the original purchaser, on a unit bought directly from us or from a listed stockist.
  • Cosmetic wear that doesn't affect function: light scuffing, minor discoloration.

If something's actually wrong.

Write to care@matchasous.com with your order confirmation, a description of the problem, and a photo or short video if the issue is visible. We'll get back to you within one business day.

If the unit is faulty through no fault of your own, we'll repair it, replace it, or refund what you paid, whichever gets you back to a working Sous soonest. That choice is ours to make, which is the nature of a limited warranty rather than a full one. It has never once meant leaving someone with a $179 paperweight.

The legal fine print.

This warranty is in addition to any implied warranties that apply under the law of your state, including the implied warranty of merchantability. We do not disclaim those. We can't, and wouldn't want to. Where the law allows it, implied warranties on this product are limited to the same one-year duration as the warranty above. Some states don't allow a limit on how long an implied warranty lasts, or the exclusion of incidental or consequential damages, so that limitation may not apply to you.

To the extent the law allows, we're not liable for incidental or consequential damages arising from a defect: lost matcha, lost time, that kind of thing. This warranty gives you specific legal rights, and you may have other rights that vary from state to state.

This warranty applies to units shipped within the United States. See the FAQ for the current shipping, cancellation and pre-order terms, which are separate from this page.
