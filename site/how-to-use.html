The guide

How to make perfect matcha.

Everything between the caddy and the cup: weighing and sifting, water and temperature, the whisk itself, then ice, milk and sweetener. Two unhurried minutes, start to sip.

The method

Six simple steps.

01

Measure

1–2 tsp of matcha — about 2–4 g. A pocket scale is the single biggest upgrade to consistency; the spoon works fine too.

02

Sift

Sift through a fine sieve straight into the vessel. Matcha compresses in the tin; sifting breaks those clumps so the vortex works fastest.

03

Fill

Water to an etched guide — 40, 80 or 120 ml. Hot bowls like water just off the boil, about 80°C / 175°F. Building an iced drink? Use less water for a concentrate.

04

Whisk

Seat the vessel and choose a preset — I gentle stir, II everyday bowl, III rich latte base — or dial any of 18 speeds. It stops itself at about 30 seconds.

05

Build

Straight: sip as is. Iced: pour the concentrate over a full glass of ice. Latte: top with hot or cold milk — oat holds the foam especially well.

06

Sweeten & finish

Honey, maple or simple syrup — stir it into the warm concentrate before the milk or ice so it dissolves fully. Taste, adjust, enjoy.

Put it to work

Three house drinks.

Same six moves, three very different cups.

i.

The straight bowl

2 g (1 tsp) sifted · 80 ml water at 80°C · Preset II.
Thirty seconds, then drink it as the tea masters intended — no additions, fine standing foam.

ii.

Iced matcha latte

3–4 g sifted · 40 ml water · Preset III for a rich base.
Sweeten the base (1–2 tsp syrup), fill a glass with ice and 200 ml cold milk, pour the base over the top.

iii.

Hot matcha latte

3 g sifted · 40 ml water at 80°C · Preset III.
Stir in honey or maple while warm, then top with 180 ml steamed or hot milk.

Care

Care takes ten seconds.

Rinse the stainless vessel and the whisk by hand — matcha releases with warm water alone. Wipe the base with a damp cloth.
