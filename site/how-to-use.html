How to make perfect matcha.

Everything between the caddy and the cup: weighing and sifting, water and temperature, the whisk itself, then ice, milk and sweetener. Two unhurried minutes, start to sip.

A change of pace, by design.

It all happens within a single programmed cycle, with no need to stop, adjust or finish the matcha yourself.

01

The faster stage

When the cycle begins, Sous creates a faster vortex that draws the sifted matcha through the water, bringing the two together evenly without requiring a whisk in the cup.

02

The finishing stage

Once that work is done, the pace shifts. The slower stage settles the larger bubbles created during mixing and leaves behind a finer, polished surface. The kind of finish that can take practice to achieve consistently by hand.

Water first. Always.

01

Add water

Pour water into the vessel before adding matcha. Use the etched 40, 80 or 120 ml guide according to your preparation. For hot matcha, around 80°C / 175°F is a useful place to begin, though a particular tea may prefer something different.

02

Sift matcha

Measure your matcha and sift it directly over the water. Water first. Matcha second.

03

Select + start

Choose a preset or adjust the speed manually, then press start. Sous begins with a faster vortex to bring the matcha and water together evenly before changing pace for the finishing stage. The cycle stops on its own.

04

Serve + enjoy

Serve it straight, build it over ice or finish it with milk. Rinse. Repeat.

Starting recipes.

i.

Matcha, straight

2 g matcha · 80 ml water.
Add the water first, sift the matcha directly over it and select your setting. Once the cycle is complete, pour and taste. Adjust the temperature or ratio according to the tea.

ii.

Iced matcha latte

4 g matcha · 40 ml water · milk · ice · sweetener if desired.
Add water first, sift in the matcha and start Sous. Build the glass with milk and ice, then pour the prepared matcha over the top.

iii.

Hot matcha latte

4 g matcha · 40 ml water · steamed milk · sweetener if desired.
Add the water first, sift in the matcha and run Sous. Finish with steamed milk and sweeten to taste, if desired.

Care takes ten seconds.

Rinse the stainless vessel and the whisk by hand. Matcha releases with warm water alone. Wipe the base with a damp cloth.
