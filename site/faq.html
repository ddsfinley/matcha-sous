Everything about living with the MS-01. Anything else — care@matchasous.com, answered within one business day.

Does the MS-01 replace a bamboo chasen?

For the result, yes. The vortex reproduces the fast, shallow agitation a chasen delivers, without the technique or fatigue. Many owners keep a chasen for occasions and use the MS-01 for every other bowl.

What matcha works best?

Any quality ceremonial or culinary matcha. Sift 1–2 teaspoons into the cup so the vortex can work fastest, and fill water to an etched 40, 80 or 120 ml guide.

What do the 18 speeds and 3 presets do?

The presets cover the common bowls: a gentle stir, an everyday bowl, and a dense latte base. The 18 manual speeds let you fine-tune agitation for volume, temperature and texture.

Can it make hot and iced matcha lattes?

Yes. Whisk matcha with a small amount of water first — the upper speeds build a dense concentrate — then pour it over hot milk or ice.

How loud is it?

It is a quiet hum, not a blender note. The whisk is driven contactlessly through the cup floor, so there is no gearbox or shaft noise.

How do I clean it?

Rinse the stainless cup and the whisk by hand and wipe the base with a damp cloth. Do not submerge the base. There are no seals, sockets or delicate parts to maintain.

How is it powered?

USB-C. A power adapter and USB-C cable ship in the case, and any common USB-C source will run it.

What ships in the case?

The mixer base, the stainless matcha cup, a power adapter, a USB-C cable and the user manual.
