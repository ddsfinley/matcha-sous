Your questions, answered.

Everything about living with Matcha Sous. Anything else, care@matchasous.com, answered within one business day.

Is Matcha Sous a milk frother?

No. A milk frother is primarily designed to introduce air. Matcha Sous runs the Sous Cycle™ instead: Incorporate brings the tea evenly through the water, then Refine changes pace to reduce larger bubbles and finish the surface.

Why does Sous use two speeds?

Because incorporating the matcha and finishing the surface are two different jobs: together they're the Sous Cycle™. A faster stage, Incorporate, brings everything together; a slower stage, Refine, settles the larger bubbles for a smoother, glossier finish. How glossy depends on the tea, the dose and the water, but the Sous Cycle™ is what gets you there.

Do I need to sift?

We recommend it. Matcha compresses easily in the tin, and sifting gives the preparation a more even starting point. With Matcha Sous, water goes into the mixing cup first, then the matcha is sifted directly over it.

Does it replace a chasen?

No. It sits beside a chasen, not instead of it. Reach for Sous when you want it hands-free, and the chasen when you don't.

Does Matcha Sous make matcha better?

It cannot improve the quality of the tea itself. What it can do is make a polished preparation easier to achieve and easier to repeat, especially when the finishing technique is something you have struggled to reproduce consistently by hand.

Can I adjust the settings?

Yes. You get three saved settings, and each one is customizable: eighteen speeds and your own run time, so you can dial in a particular tea, recipe, or exactly how much foam you want.

What are the etched markings for?

The stainless-steel mixing cup includes 40, 80 and 120 ml guides. Use less water for a concentrated latte base and more for matcha served straight.

How do I clean it?

Rinse the stainless-steel mixing cup and mixing whisk after use, then wipe the powered base with a damp cloth. Do not submerge the base.

Why is there a California Proposition 65 warning?

California requires a warning before a business exposes anyone in the state to any chemical on a list of roughly 900, at thresholds set well below the level at which harm has been observed. It is not a recall, a safety notice, or a finding about this product in particular, and the requirement applies to a great many ordinary household appliances. Because the warning attaches to the product as sold, it appears wherever the product is sold rather than only in California. The chemical we name is one associated with the flexible PVC used in cables and cords, not with the stainless-steel mixing cup or with anything that touches your matcha. You can read California’s own explanation at P65Warnings.ca.gov.

Troubleshooting.

If something's not working as expected, start here before writing in.

Sous won't turn on. What should I check?

Touch the control panel to wake it. If Power/Start is blinking, press and hold it for about 2 seconds. If nothing happens, charge it fully and test the outlet.

The display shows LO. What does that mean?

The battery is low. Charge the appliance before use.

The matcha isn't moving evenly. What's wrong?

The mixing whisk is likely missing, mis-seated, damaged, or obstructed. Switch off, inspect it without touching the sharp pin, then clean and reinstall it.

My matcha is still clumpy after a cycle. Why?

Usually the matcha went in before the water, wasn't sifted, or the dose was too high for the water volume. Add water first, sift evenly, and let the full cycle finish. If it's still clumping, reduce the matcha or add a little more water without exceeding 120 ml.

There's too much foam. How do I fix that?

Start with the factory preset that matches your total volume, then adjust one variable, speed or time, at a time rather than several at once.

Liquid is rising or spilling out of the cup. What happened?

The cup is likely overfilled, the wrong volume was selected, or something other than matcha, water or beverage syrup went in. Stop if it's safe to, let hot liquid settle, clean and dry the appliance, then restart within the 120 ml maximum.

I noticed an unusual sound, vibration or movement. What should I do?

Stop immediately. Power off, wait for movement to end, and inspect it safely. Don't continue using it if you see any damage.

Liquid got into the base or the charging port. Is that a problem?

Yes. Disconnect it from power and stop using it right away. Don't try to charge it, run it, or dry it with heat. Contact care@matchasous.com.

None of this fixed it. What now?

Stop using the appliance and write to care@matchasous.com. Don't try to disassemble or repair it yourself.
