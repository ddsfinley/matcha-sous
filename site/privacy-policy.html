Privacy policy.

What we collect, how we use it, and who we share it with. Effective August 26, 2026.

What we collect.

When you reserve a Matcha Sous or write to us, you give us information directly: your name, shipping address and email, so we can fulfil an order or answer a question. If you contact us for support, we collect whatever you choose to share about the issue, including photos or video if you send them.

When you browse the site, we collect basic technical information automatically, such as your browser and device type and which pages you visit, in the ordinary way any web server does.

Once checkout is live, our payment processor will collect what it needs to process a transaction. We do not see or store your full card details; that happens on the processor's own secure systems.

How we use it.

We use your information to process and fulfil orders, respond to questions and support requests, and improve the site. We'll send you marketing email, such as product updates or announcements, only if you've given us your consent, and every message includes a way to opt out.

Who we share it with.

We share your information with the service providers we need to run the business: our e-commerce platform, our payment processor, and the carrier that ships your order. Each only receives what it needs to do that job, and each has its own privacy policy governing how it handles your data.

We do not sell your personal information. If a link on this site takes you to a third-party site, this policy no longer applies once you're there; that site's own privacy policy governs.

Cookies and tracking.

This site does not run analytics, advertising or tracking scripts today. Once checkout is live through our e-commerce platform, it will set the essential cookies a shopping cart and checkout need to function, such as remembering what's in your cart. You can control or disable cookies in your browser's settings, though parts of checkout may not work correctly without them.

Where your data lives.

Matcha Sous runs on Shopify. Shopify provides the e-commerce platform, stores our order and customer data on its own secure servers, and processes payments on our behalf once checkout is live. When you pay by card, Shopify's payment processing is PCI-DSS compliant, the security standard set by Visa, MasterCard, American Express and Discover for handling payment data, and your transaction data is kept only as long as needed to complete the transaction.

For more detail, see Shopify's own privacy policy.

A few other things.

This site isn't directed at children, and by using it you're representing that you're at least the age of majority where you live.

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make a material change to what we collect or how we use it, we'll post the update here with a new effective date at the top of this page.

Questions about your data? Write to care@matchasous.com.
