There is matcha, and there is matcha properly prepared. Maybe you already know exactly how you like yours. Maybe you are about to discover it.

The experience

The matcha you crave. On repeat.

Good matcha begins with good tea, but the preparation shapes what finally reaches the cup. Water temperature, ratio, the way the matcha is incorporated and the finish all matter, especially when you are working with a tea worth paying attention to.

Matcha Sous was created to support that process without taking it over. You still choose the tea, decide the recipe and finish the drink the way you want it. Sous simply handles the part that is difficult to reproduce with the same consistency by hand.

For someone who already knows exactly what they like, that means an easier way back to the same cup. For someone who has never quite experienced what properly prepared matcha can be, it may be the beginning of a new standard.

How Sous prepares matcha

A change of pace, by design.

It all happens within a single programmed cycle, with no need to stop, adjust or finish the matcha yourself.

01

The faster stage

When the cycle begins, Sous creates a faster vortex that draws the sifted matcha through the water, bringing the two together evenly without requiring a whisk in the cup.

02

The finishing stage

Once that work is done, the pace shifts. The slower stage settles the larger bubbles created during mixing and leaves behind a finer, polished surface. The kind of finish that can take practice to achieve consistently by hand.

03

Press start

Sous knows when to change pace.

The method

Add water. Sift matcha. Select + start. Serve + enjoy.

The chasen

Keep the chasen.

There is a pleasure in preparing matcha entirely by hand: warming the bowl, sifting the tea, hearing bamboo against ceramic and watching the surface come together.

Matcha Sous was never meant to make that experience obsolete. It belongs beside it.

One preparation gives you the pleasure of doing every step yourself. The other gives you the pleasure of having part of that work handled for you. Both have a place.

The tea

The tea still leads.

Matcha Sous cannot change the character of the matcha you put into it, nor should it. Origin, cultivar, freshness, water temperature and ratio will always shape what you taste.

What Sous offers is a more consistent way to prepare the tea you chose, allowing those decisions to come through with fewer variables introduced at the mixing stage.

The matcha remains entirely your own. Sous simply handles its part of the preparation.

Our story

Born from repetition.

We came to Matcha Sous from behind the bar, where repetition has a way of revealing what matters.

The tea could be weighed precisely, the water measured carefully and the recipe held constant, yet the final cup could still shift depending on the hand preparing it. That variability is easy to overlook when you make one matcha at a time. During service, it becomes much harder to ignore.

We were not interested in taking craft out of matcha. We were interested in giving one of the more difficult parts of that craft better support. That became Matcha Sous.

Before you buy

Questions, answered.

Is Matcha Sous a milk frother?

No. A milk frother is primarily designed to introduce air. Matcha Sous was designed specifically around matcha preparation. The first stage brings the tea evenly through the water, while the second changes pace to reduce larger bubbles and refine the finish.

Why does Sous use two speeds?

Because the work of bringing matcha and water together is not exactly the same as the work of finishing the cup. The faster first stage handles incorporation. Once that is complete, Sous slows down to settle larger bubbles and refine the surface.

Do I need to sift?

We recommend it. Matcha compresses easily in the tin, and sifting gives the preparation a more even starting point. With Matcha Sous, water goes into the vessel first, then the matcha is sifted directly over it.

Does it replace a chasen?

No. A chasen remains an exceptional way to prepare matcha and can be part of an experience worth preserving. Sous simply offers another way to prepare matcha when you want greater repeatability, hands-free preparation or the pleasure of having that part handled for you.

Does Matcha Sous make matcha better?

It cannot improve the quality of the tea itself. What it can do is make a polished preparation easier to achieve and easier to repeat, especially when the finishing technique is something you have struggled to reproduce consistently by hand.

How do I clean it?

Rinse the stainless-steel vessel and mixing component after use, then wipe the powered base with a damp cloth. Do not submerge the base.
