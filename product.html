Matcha Sous pairs a contactless magnetic whisk with a 3-speed powered base to make café-grade matcha effortless. Add powder and water to the etched steel pitcher, set it on the base, choose your speed, and tap start. In about twenty seconds you have a smooth, foamy bowl — hot or iced — ready to pour through the drawn spout.

  • Sift 1–2 tsp matcha into the pitcher and add water or milk to the etched line.
  • Place on the base, choose speed 1–3, set the timer and tap start.
  • Pour and enjoy. Rinse the pitcher and whisk by hand; wipe the base with a damp cloth.
  • Avoid submerging the base. The pitcher and whisk are not dishwasher safe.
